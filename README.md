# Regional Public Lab Research Diversification

Dedicated canonical repository for the existing **Regional Public Research Institute / Research-Direction Diversification** project.

## Research Question

When regional public research institutes choose both research capacity and research direction, can decentralized local research choices generate excessive same-direction duplication relative to a social benchmark once correlated discovery, commercialization, and product-market substitution are taken into account?

This is a preserved research question, not a novelty-certified theorem.

## Existing Research State

Pre-existing research assets already developed a two-region public-laboratory framework separating research capacity, discovery, research direction, and commercialization. The retained candidate mechanism combines the failure-insurance value of parallel research with the possibility that decentralized commercialization releases too many substitutable successful candidates after same-direction research. A planner may therefore value diversification or selective commercialization differently from decentralized regional governments.

These statements summarize existing work only. They are not promoted here to final propositions.
