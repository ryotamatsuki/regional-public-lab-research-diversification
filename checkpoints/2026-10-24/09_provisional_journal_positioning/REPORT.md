## I. Fixed surviving contribution set (no extra mechanisms)

P1: endogenous grower conversion yields a subnational–national marginal public-quality-R&D wedge with conversion-share sign reversal, \`a*=(h+c-b)/(2h-e)\`, equals half for same-generation substitution symmetry; in interior 2-region quadratic model, conditional on SOC/global best-response/strict concavity, equilibrium effort ranking also reverses.

P2: for a separable **restricted** nonlinear demand class, wedge proportional to \`H(1-a)-H(a)\`, \`H(q)=q phi''(q)\`; increasing, constant, and decreasing H respectively preserve, neutralize and reverse the sign at symmetric interior adoption. NOT an arbitrary-demand theorem; empirical demand-elasticity sign remains unverified.

P3: exact rational example with local positive R&D Nash but globally socially optimal zero R&D, \`Delta=0.34\`, welfare loss approx .006137367 against W=18.4. Fixed R&D fee not required, BUT endogenous adoption threshold/corner and rent-shifting are essential; the example is NOT quantitatively calibrated or of demonstrated empirical economic size.

Underlying institutional game: two territorially oriented welfare-maximizing publicly funded research entities, simultaneous quality effort, competitive growers' endogenous replanting, four-variety common consumer market, endogenous prices. Not downstream Cournot or generic public/private mixed oligopoly. No endogenous licensing regime or private breeding. These omissions are bounded model assumptions, not evidence that all cases are covered.

Portability certification **working classification only**: P1 CONDITIONALLY PORTABLE to convex K, multi-region and selected geographic consumer allocations; P2 CONDITIONALLY PORTABLE only within stated nonlinear demand family; P3 MODEL-SPECIFIC existence result with verified parameter example, not a universal loss bound. Original baseline objective's robustness to funding/mission in Stage 08 is conditional and **not a new headline theorem**. Formal final classifications await Stage 7.5A.

## IV. Independent editorial value and skeptical attack

**Editor-facing value case**: Public fruit-breeding innovations change not just varietal quality but farmers' orchard composition; price-setting across old and new crops then reallocates surplus between distinct producing regions and the common consumer market. We give a tractable simultaneous decentralized-public-R&D model in which grower planting is an endogenous equilibrium response. We characterize when a subnational researcher chooses too much versus too little quality effort; reveal that a specific demand-curvature statistic controls not just magnitude but sign and even disappearance of the wedge within a stated demand family; and construct a globally verified example in which both regions optimally conduct publicly funded breeding despite aggregate welfare being maximized by no breeding. Since the agrarian licensing and public-research institutions are internationally documented, the contribution can speak to regional research-funding choices rather than solely one Japanese prefecture. The paper must not present the half-adoption crossover as an unconditional general law, or present the existence counterexample as an economically large calibrated loss. **This is a proposed editorial pitch, contingent on source-level absorption checks**.

**Strongest desk-reject argument**: Removing crop labels leaves a standard rent-shifting investment game with quadratic payoffs and a linear adoption response. The threshold at half adoption is a symmetry-generated identity, not a deep discovery, and the nonlinear curvature comparison is direct accounting of quantity-times-price-slope. Generic advertising has long demonstrated inefficient, beggar-thy-neighbor spending; welfare-immiserizing R&D under endogenous adoption also appears in other strands. The numerical zero-research counterexample rests on adoption being zero until quality effort crosses a threshold and produces a very small uncalibrated welfare loss. The paper neither identifies demand elasticities in real fruit markets nor derives an actionable cooperative funding scheme. A field editor could rationally determine that, despite valid proofs, the paper's economic contribution remains below the journal's threshold. These are substantive risks, not refutations, and warrant direct audit rather than abstract optimism.

**Question completeness / WHY-SHOULD-COULD**: WHY = producer income/consumer incidence local-nation wedge established; SHOULD = constrained coordinated national research benchmark established; COULD = implementable national funding transfer/contract not modeled as part of frozen 3 propositions; can remain limited only if the journal accepts diagnosis-only applied theory backed by source comparable papers. A pure theory article does NOT inherently require an additional instrument or real data, but **policy-oriented journal may**; benchmark alone should not be labeled an available policy.
