This defeats an originality argument based merely on land-share conversion, regional specialty licensing, quality-differentiated agricultural products, competitive growers, and producer/consumer welfare accounting. These are ALREADY jointly modeled in prestigious agricultural economics.

### Exact source theorem comparison

Lence et al. working paper **Proposition 1:** orders the maximum viable **fixed development costs** across organizational regimes (perfect competition, supply-control organizations, monopoly, social planner). **P2:** conditional on development, compares society's welfare surplus between regimes. **P3:** there is a nonempty development-cost range in which competitive producer organization cannot fund development while an organization with market power can and improves social welfare.

Their model's producer development decision is PRIVATE/PARTICIPATORY and maximizes total producer surplus *given organizational control*, rather than two regional public entities simultaneously choosing smooth quality R&D under regional objectives. Their original x denotes FARM OUTPUT PER ACRE, not quality-R&D expenditure; a is farm land share. Their result that welfare gain is positive if the producer organization voluntarily invests uses the identity **Delta SS=Delta PS+CS**, with CS>0 under their market assumptions, and development only if Delta PS exceeds fixed K. They assume the original commodity price is perfectly elastic for the studied local group and the wider commodity market is negligible to this group. In our common 4-good national market, cross-region producer price spillovers can be negative and are counted in W. The two statements are therefore NOT logically contradictory; their sufficient conditions are absent in the candidate.

**Classification:** constituent features are closely anticipated; exact model/game is **NOT directly isomorphic** to the Lence 2006 proposition system. Whether another generic parent theorem implies the new research-incidence threshold remains unresolved; no automatic novelty certification is allowed.
