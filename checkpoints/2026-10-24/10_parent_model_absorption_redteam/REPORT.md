## B. Independently reproduced algebraic absorption mapping

Write quantities as q=q0+Sa with q0=(1,0,1,0) and

\[
S=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\1&0\\0&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
p=V_q,\qquad V=\alpha(x)^\top q-q^\top Bq/2.
\]

Stage-2 farmers' interior best response is a(x)=H^{-1}(z 1+x), H=[[d,e],[e,d]], d=kappa+2h, D=d²-e². Thus adoption derivatives for a unilateral i research increase are d/D for own and -e/D for the other.

At *fixed* adoption, because the quality shifter raises only price of own new variety without altering consumer surplus at fixed q, local and national research derivatives are both a_i-Rx_i; **wedge exactly zero**. This is solely a falsification diagnostic and NOT a separate core model.

With endogenous competitive replanting, the research-mediated price response is

\[
p_{a\leftarrow x_i}=-BS\,a_{x_i}.
\]

The entire subnational–national research wedge at the symmetric interior point becomes the **standard producer-price-incidence identity**

\[
G_{i,x_i}-W_{x_i}=q_i^\top p_{a\leftarrow x_i}-\frac12q^\top p_{a\leftarrow x_i}.
\]

Expanding the four prices and land shares yields **exactly** P1:

\[
{h-j-(2h-e)a\over2L}.
\]

Thus the generalized price-income/envelope decomposition is **not new mathematics**. The precise fixed-land induced, decentralized public-R&D equilibrium crossing, its interpretation, and its status vs named previous THEOREMS must be judged separately. P2's q-times-price-slope curvature condition is a restricted-demand corollary of the same identity. P3 is a genuinely checked existence example in this game but classic rent-shifting excessive spending has precedent.
