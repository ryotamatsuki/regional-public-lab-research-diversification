## Canonical game fingerprint and exact non-mapping

**Our game:** first stage two public agencies simultaneously choose local quality effort x_i>=0 and maximize G_i=Pi_i+CS/2-R*x_i^2/2; second stage competitive growers choose 0<=a_i<=1 old-to-new conversion with \`q_iO=1-a_i,q_iN=a_i\`; net surplus \`Pi_i=p_i'q_i-K(a_i)\`; national W=Pi_1+Pi_2+CS-R sum x_i²/2. Downstream price vector four goods \`p=alpha(x)-Bq\`. Farmer FOC \`H a=z 1+x\` with H=[[d,e],[e,d]]; KKT boundaries matter.

**AFJ:** first-stage different industry producer groups maximize own producer-group profit (not local consumer surplus), demands for linked goods, unlinked supply in explicit eq(2), cooperative planner maximizes aggregate producer surplus. Cannot map regional KKT adopting one old into one new plus national CS objective by a trivial relabel of eq(1)–(3); but sufficiently broad generalization of AFJ could nest us, so full theorem absorption unresolved.

**Balagtas/Kim:** common dairy farmer profit program chooses multiple promotional activities **jointly**, while factor allocation at downstream processing level is endogenous; no strategic territorial public-welfare Nash first stage. Nonidentical joint payoff/timing, but conversion and cross-market incidence are known. A Nash multi-region variant built as extension may contain our result; exact existing-paper theorem does not explicitly state it in checked draft eqs(39)–(48).
