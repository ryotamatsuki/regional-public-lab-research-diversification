## Final decision

**PRIMARY: Australian Journal of Agricultural and Resource Economics (AJARE)**. **Native LaTeX .tex + PDF initial submissions are expressly supported** in Wiley Research Exchange; author uploads `Main Document – LaTeX .tex File` and `Main Document – LaTeX PDF`, with BibTeX/supporting sources in LaTeX Supplementary File. No submission fee. Ordinary, non-open-access subscription publishing does not trigger the optional Open Access APC. Current author guide lists no compulsory page fee (but seek one explicit editorial confirmation if fees ever change). Well-established field research journal, with both theoretical and empirical content. Publisher currently emphasizes original novel quantitative policy-relevant work, and **prefers 6,000–10,000 words inclusive**. Thus **substantial desk-rejection risk** from limited economic-theory novelty, no empirical calibration, and a roughly 4–5k-word brief theoretical manuscript. First submission is defensible only as a deliberate *stretch* that avoids needless format conversion, not an estimated high probability of acceptance.

**SECOND / CONDITIONAL: Agricultural and Resource Economics Review (ARER)**. Explicit LaTeX author path: upload compiled PDF as Main Article and full `*.tex`/`*.bib` sources as ZIP; NO Word. The journal is fully Gold OA; Cambridge says **authors outside publication agreements without APC funding can obtain an APC waiver**. Zero author cost is therefore **conditional on actual lack of funds/eligibility**, not blanket Diamond OA. Research Article is normally ~10k words including references; Research Insights <=3k words, NOT currently feasible without sacrificing or relocating proof-critical material. Its editorial potential may be more realistic than AJARE's, but no guaranteed waiver or positive desk decision. If free publishing is guaranteed only with no application/waiver, ARER is removed and the third journal becomes second.

**THIRD / STRETCH: Agricultural Economics (Wiley/IAAE)**. Publisher explicitly permits **Word or LaTeX main source on initial submission**, anonymized, and converts submissions to referee PDF. Maximum 10k words/word-equivalents, **no page charges**, and optional paid hybrid OA; choose ordinary subscription publishing to avoid OA APC. Official source did not explicitly state whether there are any additional submission charges; **do not falsely certify their absence without checking directly at submission**. Flagship of IAAE; seeks strong applied economic theoretical contribution with broad decision-making implications, stronger value-added bar than this current narrowly institution-specific one-parameter-family diagnostic. Its data/code policy requires archival repository deposition (Stage9/GitHub proof script route supports it); journal Abstract/highlights and named section conventions also differ from Stage13 BAE. Desk risk **VERY HIGH** given Stage 6 and 11 novelty limitations. Not an easier fallback solely because .tex accepted.

**JAFIO (Journal of Agricultural & Food Industrial Organization; De Gruyter Brill): HOLD / POSSIBLE BETTER CONTENT MATCH, NOT YET CERTIFIED FOR THIS HARD GATE.** Official aims explicitly welcome theoretical agricultural/food IO, differentiated products, innovation and industry policy, plus concise notes; content and article length may fit the frozen paper better than AJARE/AGEC. Official publisher author-instructions PDF (last updated 2024-06-10) shows LaTeX editable tables and optional ordinary subscription publishing **with no hybrid OA APC**, but **its exact initial .tex-source upload requirement or acceptance could not be independently confirmed** because the publisher PDF endpoint returned HTTP 405 through web.open. A PDF prepared by LaTeX is not, without further evidence, proof of full LaTeX-source acceptance. No mandatory submission/page fee was identified on the journal overview; the editor should confirm before putting it above AJARE/ARER. No claim of verified journal-specific initial LaTeX, free route, or expected acceptance.

**Disqualified under hard native-TeX + cost criteria:**
- **Bio-based and Applied Economics (BAE)**: although agricultural game theory and zero-fee Diamond OA fit well, its official submissions page specifies editable Word/OpenOffice/RTF and does not authorize `paper.tex` as the main journal manuscript. Withdraw as first choice **solely due to format**, not a change in the model.
- **Italian Review of Agricultural Economics (REA)**: its mandatory title/manuscript **template** and TNR-12 formatting do not expressly accept LaTeX source; exact initial .tex upload **not verified**. Diamond free alone insufficient.
- **Review of Agricultural, Food and Environmental Studies (RAFE)**: theoretical agricultural research + no-APC standard subscription hybrid route fit; refreshed Cambridge journal-specific 'Preparing your materials' instructions **do not expressly enumerate LaTeX upload**, so it is not certified; additionally Editorial Manager unavailable **2026-10-26 to 2026-11-16** during publisher transfer. Can return to shortlist only after journal office gives explicit source acceptance confirmation.
- **Journal of Agricultural Economics (JAE; Wiley)**: .tex explicitly accepted and no submission/page charges, **BUT at least one author must hold paid Agricultural Economics Society membership before submission** absent editor-approved hardship exemption or LMIC exemption. The author is not assumed to qualify; this fails the strict automatic-zero-cash requirement. Also higher editorial novelty bar.
- **Journal of Agricultural and Resource Economics (JARE; U.S.)**: accepts LaTeX among native source files but compulsory accepted-page fees ($79/page member, $89/page nonmember) fail the no-mandatory-fees hard gate.
