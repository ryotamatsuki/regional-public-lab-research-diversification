# JAFIO — Author Fee Verification From Official Publisher Sources

**Verified:** 2026-10-25 JST  
**Article:** Regional Public Breeding and Variety Replacement  
**Request:** Verify all mandatory author charges before selecting Journal of Agricultural & Food Industrial Organization (JAFIO) for a direct LaTeX submission.
**This checkpoint updates the fees-only uncertainty of Stage 12-M/Checkpoint 41.** No article, theory or journal submission was changed.

## Finding / confidence

**CONFIRMED: Non-open-access/subscription publication incurs NO author publication charges.** Not a mere inference from the word "hybrid":

1. The journal-specific **JAFIO Instructions for Authors, last updated 2025-10-11**, publisher PDF indexed full-text at https://www.degruyterbrill.com/publication/journal_key/JAFIO/downloadAsset/JAFIO_Instructions%20for%20Authors%20JAFIO.pdf, p.6 under "Article Processing Charges (APCs)" states that authors who do not choose OA pay **no APC**, only an author who opts for OA incurs an APC, and the choice is made AFTER acceptance and BEFORE production. A free typeset author PDF is stated in same document.
2. Publisher FAQ https://www.degruyterbrill.com/publishing/about-us/new-website-faqs/authors-editors explicitly answers the author-charge question: **"De Gruyter authors pay nothing for publication in subscription-based journals."** This is a broad *publication-fee* affirmation, covering standard production rather than just the optional Gold-OA APC.
3. Official JAFIO homepage https://www.degruyterbrill.com/journal/key/jafio/html says **upon acceptance authors HAVE THE OPTION** to publish OA; non-OA articles exist and repository policies differentiate them. The normal route is a subscription-based publication, not a fully OA-only journal.
4. The 2024-06-10 JAFIO Instructions for Authors give the same APC policy, corroborating that this is not an accidental 2025 wording.

### Fee-by-fee decision matrix

| Fee component | Evidence and decision | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription/non-Gold publication charge | 2025 JAFIO-specific APC policy plus publisher-wide FAQ explicitly says authors pay nothing in subscription journals | **CONFIRMED $0** |
| Optional Gold OA Article Processing Charge | Charged **only on opting in after acceptance**, exact journal amount not located in accessible official pricing; author may decline | **AVOIDABLE**, exact amount not verified and **not needed** |
| Page/production/composition charges | JAFIO-specific indexed 2025 author-guide fee section does not list such charges, and publisher FAQ says zero for normal publication; no separate JAFIO page fee located | **Strong support for $0**, but the absence of a standalone journal-specific itemized sentence is not positive proof of every conceivable special service charge |
| Initial submission/handling/review fee | JAFIO homepage and indexed 2025 instructions show online submission/review but contain **no explicit sentence saying "no submission fee"**; no charge located in publisher or secondary searches | **No fee identified; not independently certified** |
| Mandatory society membership | No membership requirement identified in publisher journal description / indexed guide; do not assume unspecified memberships | **No requirement identified** |
| Optional author-selected services, paid print copies, paid OA | Not included in no-charge standard publication decision | **Exclude by declining**, if offered |

**Strict financial recommendation:** Strong enough to treat **publication under the standard subscription path as $0 by publisher policy** and to keep JAFIO the content-merit-balanced first candidate. It would be inaccurate to maintain "APC-free standard JAFIO publication is unverified"; now VERIFIED. Equally inaccurate to claim **a documentary unconditional guarantee of the absence of a distinct submission fee**: neither guide nor homepage explicitly says "submission fee zero." Given the user's zero-cash hard requirement, request a single specific clarification before clicking final journal SUBMIT, if an absolute pre-submission guarantee is desired.
