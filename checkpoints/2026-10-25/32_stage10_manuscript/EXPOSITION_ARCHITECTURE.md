Profile: **THEORY-FIRST**, compact original applied-theory **Research Article**, not a new general IO theory paper. Journal is not selected; no invented page/word threshold.

## Reader-arrival and section-purity budget

1. First introduction paragraph: regionally funded public cultivar breeding and the national-welfare question, supported by OECD institutional reference rather than invented institutional facts.
2. Second paragraph: one-stage funding vs subsequent competitive adoption, principal sign-dependent result, and exact positive-local/zero-national counterexample.
3. Third paragraph: four most relevant comparators—Alston et al. (promotion overinvestment), Balagtas & Kim (multi-product input reallocation), Lence et al. (differentiated products/collective marketing) and Akhundjanov et al. (public university apple licensing)—and *narrow* contribution difference. No general first-ever research-overinvestment claim.
4. Section 2: complete consumer utility/price primitives, land and continuous research choices, observation/timing, local 1/2 CS objective, national criterion, competitive grower KKT including corners. This is the only full baseline setup.
5. Section 3: P1 wedge with visible producer-income/CS price-incidence bridge, conditional equilibrium gap, P2 restricted nonlinear model and essential demand-symmetry limit.
6. Section 4: exact P3+ global positive Nash vs zero planner, all three adoption regimes, primitive-based regional gain and nationally real-resource square completion. The headline result appears before the robustness/discussion.
7. Section 5: bounded failed and successful robustness, separate nonzero-e coupled case, institutional omissions.
8. Discussion/Conclusion: welfare diagnostic only; no enforceable policy instrument, no empirical budget recommendation.
9. Appendices: nonlinear utility-to-price-to-Jacobian incidence; global boundary branch inequalities; economic-to-formal proof crosswalk with exact Lean scope.

## Five-exhibit gate

- Equations: necessary, kept near theorem.
- Main table: **not required**; would largely duplicate explicit P1 threshold and exact P3 result.
- Main figure: **not required**; purely symbolic model, no meaningful empirical scaling and no new economic logic beyond the displayed branch formulas.
- Numerical examples: concise inline verified exact rationals, not a pseudo-calibration.
- All comparative statics, welfare and scope have **one primary vehicle**: P1 proposition, P2 restricted proposition, P3 global proposition, short robustness paragraphs, respectively.
