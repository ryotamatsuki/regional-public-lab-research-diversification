## Referee risks and editorial response

1. **Known parent mechanism.** Noncooperative promotion/research spending and product-market incidence already explain excessive spending. Openly acknowledge these ancestors. The contribution must be the precise institutional game and results, not an invention of socially harmful R&D.
2. **Weak fiscal microfoundation.** Equal halves of national consumer surplus are assumed rather than derived from residence or intergovernmental finance. Explain why this defines a transparent benchmark; do not call it empirically representative.
3. **Main example has independent best responses.** With e=j=0, payoff interdependence does not create strategic complementarity/substitutability of effort. Keep this distinction explicit. Interacting examples supply examples only, not general uniqueness.
4. **Four goods and deterministic innovation.** The crop interpretation is useful but disease protection, adaptation, risk, seed licensing and dynamic orchards are absent. Do not present the zero national optimum as a finding about actual breeding budgets.
5. **Curvature result is restricted.** P2 should not be advertised as arbitrary nonlinear-demand robustness. Its common-curvature assumptions and counterexample are substantive.
6. **Certification can distract.** Lean and symbolic checks help reproducibility; a referee must reconstruct the proofs in the paper. File names and internal stage terminology should be removed from the economic narrative where they interrupt it.
7. **No calibrated importance.** Exact welfare loss proves possibility, not magnitude or prevalence. Explain why evaluating variety uptake alone can miss an upstream research distortion; avoid invented optimal coordination/subsidy policies.
8. **AI eligibility.** Substantive drafting, search and code/proof assistance require truthful disclosure and actual human accountability. Correct mathematics and an AI statement do not certify the publisher accepts this manuscript's production process.

Supply the verified primitive vector or reorganize around the fully specified second interacting benchmark; no new result is required.
3. The AI disclosure's “previously specified theory-review scope” is an internal-process reference that is undefined for a referee. State precisely what the available author attestation covers, while preserving the honest limitation on documented independent human rederivation. Do not strengthen the attestation.

No new exaggeration of strategic interaction, nonlinear generality or policy optimality was identified. The three policy-evaluation distinctions are appropriately conditional. Formal-certification sentences still cost some reading space, but their explicit scope is appropriate given the risk of overstating what was verified.

Subject to resolving the three points, **narrow editorial readiness can PASS**, while **journal suitability/novelty remains CONDITIONAL**, because known parent mechanisms, assumed welfare weights and inaccessible close-comparator full texts remain material editorial risks.
