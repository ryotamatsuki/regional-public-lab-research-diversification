# Bibliography audit — Stage 13J / 14J

Date: 2026-10-25 JST. Scope: all 14 existing records, not a new novelty search.

## Method and result

Publisher-deposited DOI metadata was retrieved live from the Crossref works API for 13 records; the AgEcon Search DOI is registered outside Crossref and was checked against its repository record and the journal’s own article page. `crossref_metadata.json` preserves the successful API fields (and the expected Crossref 404 for the repository DOI). Initial 429 responses for Alston et al. and Akhundjanov et al. were resolved by sequential retry. No DOI, author-order, title, journal, volume, issue, publication-year or page correction is required. Title capitalization and hyphen typography are stylistic rather than identity differences.

This is a bibliographic identity audit. It does not certify every substantive interpretation of each paper or assert full-text access to all 14 papers. The manuscript citation-key bijection is separately checked by `verify_jafio.py`.

## Action for the editor

Retain all 14 existing entries. Do not change correct issue years to DOI online/backfile years. Do not remove Foster’s documented middle initial simply because Crossref has a shorter display name. Preserve the protected R&D and Honeycrisp capitalization and the LaTeX accents for Stéphan Marette and Metin Çakır. DOI case is immaterial.

Any newly added Winfree–McCluskey entry must be checked separately for online-versus-issue year and article number; the JAFIO-fit audit owns that addition and the substantive comparison.
