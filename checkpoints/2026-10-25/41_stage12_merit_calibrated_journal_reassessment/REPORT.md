## Decision

**First candidate: Journal of Agricultural & Food Industrial Organization (JAFIO)**, *conditional on direct confirmation of zero obligatory non-OA submission, page and publication costs*. This supersedes the former AJARE-first selection (checkpoint 40). The reason is a materially better alignment of the actual narrowly institutional economic research contribution with the journal's observed theory and agricultural-sector article bar.

**Second candidate: Agricultural and Resource Economics Review (ARER)** only if author has no relevant APC funding and can obtain the Cambridge waiver; actual LaTeX submission is expressly documented. Full Research Article route rather than its <=3000-word Research Insight.

**Third candidate: Australian Journal of Agricultural and Resource Economics (AJARE)** as an explicitly ambitious stretch, not an easy or most likely acceptance outcome. Direct LaTeX and no submission charges are publisher verified, ordinary subscription publication avoids optional OA APC; preferred manuscript range 6000–10000 words and stronger generalizable economics/policy contribution than the current narrow diagnostic case.

**Fourth fallback: Agricultural Economics Research Review (AERR, Sage)** if lower standing is acceptable. Publisher explicitly accepts LaTeX and says no fees to submit or publish. Long-running Indian professional research journal (1988 inception) and ABDC C per an external journal list. Current articles are overwhelmingly context-specific applied empirical studies; the journal is not a like-for-like substitute for a well-established internationally recognized IO/field theory journal. Do not conflate with IAAE/Wiley Agricultural Economics.

**On hold:** German Journal of Agricultural Economics (GJAE) is Diamond with verified agricultural field publishing and Scopus/publisher IF ~1.5, but the journal-specific initial full .tex upload pathway is unverified; do not borrow TeX permission from TIB's different proceedings journals. IAAE Agricultural Economics has explicit TeX but demands much broader contribution. BAE has high theory-sector fit, but no accepted raw LaTeX submission path. JARE has mandatory page fees.

## Actual economic quality of present research

**High technical rigor relative to its scoped claims:** Original quasilinear four-variety consumer-price primitives; public-quality leaders and efficient competitive grower constrained switchers; exact local-national welfare externality; full three-planting-regime global investment best responses and globally unique no-research planner for a precisely stated parameter interval. Proof-critical core Lean, independent SymPy, strong counterexample/boundary tests, and separate rational coupled e>0 calculation. This distinguishes rigor from economic novelty.

**Modest general theory originality:** Consumer-incidence identity dG_i/dx_i - dW/dx_i = -dG_j/dx_i, Beggar-Thy-Neighbor producer/promotion spending and socially negative investment incentives are known. P1 threshold exists under a special symmetric interior parameterization; P2 neutrality fails with unequal old/new curvature. Exact P3+ globally verified is in e=j=0 family with independent research best-response functions (no strict cross-region BR strategic dependence); e>0 example is not a new general Nash theorem. Regional governments value one-half of national CS by assumption; actual financial, patent, uncertainty and farmer-vintage institutions are excluded.

**Positive economic learning at one institutional boundary:** Downstream price-taking growers can make an efficient fixed-quality crop conversion yet regional publicly funded quality research can be individually rational and reduce national real welfare. A clean precise agricultural public-R&D application including adoption-sensitive signed incidence and global example without fixed-cost invention or farmer monopoly. This supports a **compact agriculture/food institutional theory paper**, not a novel general industrial organization theorem or a policy-ready empirical estimate.

**Expected editorial issue:** Economic novelty and institutional realism can still prompt rejection even at JAFIO. No mathematical correctness/CI green result implies acceptance. The journal specifically requires issue-driven agricultural/food-sector practical relevance; explain actual public-breeding incentive design but avoid claiming an unmodeled coordination policy.
