# Provenance

## Project Origin

This repository consolidates pre-existing research on public research institutes / public experimental laboratories, cultivar or variety breeding, decentralized regional R&D, endogenous research-direction choice, correlated discovery, commercialization, and downstream competition.

The project predates this repository. A full manuscript/PDF and source-level research assets existed by 2026-07-28.

## Existing Model Work

Recovered research records describe a model with two regional public laboratories. The work separates research capacity, discovery, research direction, and commercialization. Same-direction projects can have more correlated discovery and yield more substitutable successful products. Parallel research also provides failure-insurance value. Decentralized regional commercialization and a social planner can therefore value research-direction configurations differently.

A pre-existing implementation result candidate considered coordinated public rights management, including an off-path duplicate-direction fee, as a possible support for mediated diversification. This repository does not newly derive or certify that result.
