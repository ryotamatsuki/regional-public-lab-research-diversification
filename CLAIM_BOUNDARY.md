# Claim Boundary

## Allowed at the Current Stage

- Research-direction diversification is a **candidate mechanism** already present in the research record.
- Decentralized versus social research-direction choice is under investigation.
- Existing work considers capacity, correlated discovery, commercialization, and product-market substitution as distinct margins.
- Exact novelty remains unresolved.
- The project has enough pre-existing substance to justify a dedicated repository and an exact prior-art re-kill.

## Not Allowed at the Current Stage

Do not claim that:

- this repository contains a novel theorem;
- the public-research-institute setting itself creates novelty;
- a planner generally prefers diversification;
- decentralized governments necessarily overduplicate research;
- generic R&D duplication, business stealing, or portfolio diversification is novel;
- prior literature does not contain the mechanism;
- the old `decentralized_r_and_d` paper validates or supplies novelty for this project; or
- the pre-existing implementation idea is already a publication-grade mechanism-design result.

## Kill Discipline

If label stripping reduces the contribution to a standard R&D-specialization, duplication, portfolio, research-direction, innovation-race, public-R&D, or product-market-cannibalization result, the project should be killed or materially reframed rather than rescued through labels.
