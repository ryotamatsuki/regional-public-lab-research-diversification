## Core Research Question

When regional public research institutes choose research direction as well as research capacity, under what conditions do decentralized regional choices differ from the socially desirable allocation of research directions once discovery correlation, commercialization, and downstream product-market substitution are accounted for?

## Current Candidate Mechanism

Existing work separates three margins: research capacity, discovery, and commercialization. Same-direction research can increase correlation in discovery and generate more substitutable successful candidates, while parallel research also provides failure-insurance value. Regional governments may place greater weight on local producer rents, whereas a social planner internalizes broader consumer-surplus, displacement, duplication, and portfolio effects.

A pre-existing implementation idea also considered coordinated rights management / an off-path duplicate-direction fee as a way to support mediated diversification. This is preserved as an existing result candidate, not certified here as novel or final.
